# Phishing, Fake Pages & Social Engineering

## 1. What I Learned
Phishing is a deceptive attempt to trick users into revealing credentials, codes, personal data, or access by pretending to be trusted.

It is a form of social engineering, which means manipulating people instead of directly attacking systems.

## 2. Why It Matters
Even strong passwords and 2FA can fail if a user is tricked into giving access to a fake page or fraudulent message.

Phishing awareness is one of the most practical and important personal security skills.

## 3. Practical Exploration
I reviewed how phishing attempts often rely on:

- urgency
- trust
- fake login pages
- suspicious domains
- social manipulation

I also practiced checking real login URLs and improving my “pause before login” habit.

## 4. Key Takeaway
- Phishing attacks trust more than technology
- A page can look real and still be fake
- Verification matters more than visual appearance

## 5. Tools / Concepts Used
- Domain awareness
- Login verification
- Social engineering awareness
- Trust evaluation
