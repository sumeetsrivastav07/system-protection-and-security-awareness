# Trackers and Web Privacy

## 1. What I Learned

Web trackers are mechanisms used to collect information about browsing activity.

Tracking can involve cookies, scripts, tracking pixels, analytics services, advertising services, and other third-party resources.

A webpage can communicate with multiple domains besides the main website.

## 2. Why It Matters

Understanding tracking helps explain browser privacy, cookie behavior, third-party requests, analytics, and advertising systems.

It also helps developers understand what information their websites may expose to external services.

## 3. Practical Exploration

Used Chrome DevTools Network tab to observe requests made while loading a webpage.

Compared first-party and third-party domains.

Inspected cookie properties through:

Application → Storage → Cookies

Observed cookie attributes such as Domain, Path, Secure, HttpOnly, and SameSite.

## 4. Key Takeaway

- Websites can communicate with multiple services while loading.
- Cookies are not automatically malicious.
- Third-party requests and tracking should be understood in terms of privacy and data exposure.
